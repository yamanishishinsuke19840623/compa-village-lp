Our Story

Beyond hitchhiking and skateboarding —
the place we found.

Our host is a traveler through and through — hitchhiked across America, skateboarded the length of Japan. Somewhere along the way, Shimonoseki, Yamaguchi caught his heart. Riding that momentum, he opened a guesthouse right here.

"A place to breathe easy for travelers with nowhere else to call home.
A space where pets — family members too — can stay together."

What you'll find here isn't hotel-style service, but a human touch and travel memories. Having been travelers ourselves, we value the kind of easy distance and warmth that only fellow travelers understand. Sip your morning coffee while watching pets play in the garden, cook together while catching up with new friends — or simply enjoy some quiet time alone. All of it is welcome here.

From the host: "Let's all spend the best time together — the kind you can only have out in the countryside!"

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in / check-out times?

Check-in is 16:00–22:00, and check-out is by 10:00. If you need a different time, please let us know via a special request when booking (not guaranteed).

Can I park on-site?

Yes, a private parking area is available free of charge.

Is breakfast provided?

Breakfast is not provided, but you're welcome to cook for yourself in the shared kitchen.

Are there rooms with a private bathroom?

The Standard Twin and Dormitory rooms use a shared bathroom. With the Group Plan (private use of the ground floor), you'll have a private bathroom.

What's the WiFi policy?

Free high-speed WiFi (295Mbps) is available with no restrictions in every room and shared space.

Is there a restaurant?

There's no restaurant on-site, but you can cook in the shared kitchen or enjoy a BBQ in the garden (¥2,000/group).

Is there an airport shuttle?

No shuttle service is available. The nearest station is JR Shin-Shimonoseki.

Is there a spa or pool?

Neither is available, but enjoy your time in nature instead — a walking trail in the hills behind us, BBQs in the garden, or a movie night.

Are towels and sheets included?

Towels and sheets are available for an additional fee.

Is English support available?

Yes, we can support you in English — travelers from overseas are very welcome.
